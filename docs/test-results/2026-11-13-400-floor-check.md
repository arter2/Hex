# 400-floor check, v312 against v301

Date: 2026-11-13 · For: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-09-dungeon-look-speed-variety-design.md (piece 3 checks)

400 seeded floors, depths 1 to 24 in turn (all six areas), each built the way a new game builds it, on v312 and on
v301 (the plan's base build) on the same machine. No touch play this time: floors were built and checked, not walked.

| Check | v312 | v301 |
|---|---|---|
| Floors built, no page errors | 400 / 400 | 400 / 400 |
| Every main room reachable on foot | 400 / 400 | 400 / 400 |
| Stairs down reachable | 400 / 400 | 400 / 400 |
| Rooms a floor (12 to 16 wanted) | 12 to 16, mean 14.3 | 12 to 16, mean 14.0 |
| Planned puzzle rooms placed | 192 / 192 | 178 / 192 |
| Planned special rooms placed | 169 / 172 (98%) | 128 / 172 (74%) |
| Planned lairs placed | 58 / 58 | 51 / 58 |
| Placed roles in shaped rooms (a third wanted) | 79% | 0% |
| Puzzle kinds seen | 20 | 14 |

- The three special rooms not placed were a Collapsed bridge (needs a room a chasm can split with its ledge clear of
  doors) and two Switch circuits (they give up when locking the room would cut off the stairs). Both are by design.
- Eight floors have a room with a pocket behind clutter and a chest in it: all eight are the Collapsed bridge's ledge,
  which is reached once the planks are laid. With the bridge laid every chest is reachable.

## Build time

Run one at a time on the same 100 floors (nothing else running), after keeping an area's painted floors for its next
floor (nature.js `XAREA_FLOOR_KEEP`):

| | median | 90th percentile | worst |
|---|---|---|---|
| v312 | 926 ms | 1493 ms | 2509 ms |
| v301 | 1082 ms | 1384 ms | 1838 ms |

- Typical floors are faster than v301; the 90th percentile is within 8%.
- The worst case misses the plan's 20%: it is the first floor of an area, which paints that area's floors (the
  Hollows' 512-pixel cobbles take most of it) and its theme floors. Every later floor of the area reuses them.
- In the four-at-once run (all 400 floors on each build, sharing the machine) single floors spiked to 9 to 12 s on
  v312 and to 8.7 s on v301: that was the shared machine, not the game; the same floors alone take 1 to 2.5 s.

## Painting the next area ahead (after this check)

On the last floor of an area, once the stairs down have been seen and you are within a dozen cells of them, the next
area's floors and its themes' floors are painted one at a time, each after you have stood still for 0.6 s
(nature.js `xPrewarmTick`). Measured on each area change, same machine:

| Change | Longest single step (standing still) | Next area's first floor builds in |
|---|---|---|
| Hollows → Deeps (4 → 5) | 148 ms | 902 ms |
| Deeps → Vault (8 → 9) | 161 ms | 974 ms |
| Vault → Rifts (12 → 13) | 141 ms | 861 ms |
| Rifts → Ruins (16 → 17) | 87 ms | 956 ms |
| Ruins → Abyss (20 → 21) | 68 ms | 968 ms |

Without it the Deeps' first floor took 1264 ms. An area's first floor now builds like any other, within the plan's
20% of v301. The one floor not covered is the first of a run, reached from camp: nothing has been painted yet.
