# Revision2 review notes

Tile changes: Deeps has silver metal flecks, Ruins uses aged ivory-grey marble, Abyss has gold metal flecks. Their PBR and variation maps were regenerated from the corresponding revised art. Metalness is1 only on the silver/gold flecks and0 on the surrounding stone. Marble centers use lower roughness than worn rims.

Selected upright foliage sheets were edited from the exact selected images, then corrected to a mixed plant layout. All six upright sheets now have THREE FERNS and THREE GRASS TUFTS per area, plus four crystal clusters, four fungi clusters and two roots. The final plant silhouettes are simplified for small-scale readability and use broad complementary triad colour groups. No cereal heads or wheat.

The tiny-size proof is deliberately honest:4px is a coloured clump,8px is marginal for fern-versus-grass identity,12px is more readable. These are flat compositing/resampling studies, not in-game screenshots. The code has not been changed and the material has not been integrated or performance-tested in the game.

The material maps and new silhouettes replace their earlier versions in the two full packages. The original art brief is preserved for provenance; where it conflicts with these explicit revisions, this file and the user changes take precedence.
