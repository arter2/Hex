# Cards with the same rules

Date: 2026-10-17 · Source: every card in v77 (`CARD_LIST`), heroes left out (1865 cards)

## How cards were compared

Two cards have **the same rules** when they are the same colour and do the same thing: the same kind of card, the same
shape, pattern, behaviour or effect (for example "Summon: shooter" or "Lob: burst, +burn"), the same keywords (burn, stun, push...)
and the same race tag, since races boost each other in your hand. Only their numbers, name, rank and rarity differ.
Most of these cards were made by the generator, so many share a rule and differ only in power.

## Summary

- **910** different rules across the 1865 cards.
- **955** cards repeat a rule another card of their colour already has.
- Keeping **one card per rule per rarity** would leave **1256** cards (cutting 609).
- Keeping **one card per rule** would leave **910** cards.
