# 3D Battle Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Draw the battle in three.js with the dungeon's lighting, materials and settings, in an
area-themed arena with cinematic camera moves and spell lights.

**Architecture:**

- `look.js` takes the shared look out of `explore.js`.
- `arena.js` builds and runs the 3D battle scene.
- `battle.js`'s `render()` keeps computing every tile's state colour and every unit's draw request.
  It hands these to `arena.js` and only draws flat overlays (bars, labels, numbers, previews,
  particles) on the 2D `#board` canvas, which now sits transparent over a new `#board3d` WebGL
  canvas.

**Tech Stack:** three.js r128 (vanilla globals), Playwright with headless Chromium for checks.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-14-3d-battle-design.md`

**Deviation from spec (decided while planning):** the battle gets its own `WebGLRenderer` on
`#board3d` instead of moving the dungeon's canvas between screens.

- Moving the canvas would also move its dungeon pointer listeners into the battle.
- three.js keeps GPU state per renderer, so the same textures, materials and shader patches work
  in both renderers. Only one of them draws at a time.

## Global Constraints

- No pinch zoom. Point 9 (polish and accessibility) stays deferred.
- `proj(wx,y,wz)`, `scaleAt(wx,wz)`, `hexCorners`, `pickAt` and `View.iy` keep their names and
  meanings (canvas pixels).
- World units equal battle units: one hex has radius 1, and tile tops are at y = 0.
- One copy of `XLIT` / `XLITU`: debug changes apply to both screens.
- Every round: commit, push to the branch and to main, build `hexmancers-itch-vN.zip` from
  `v8list.txt` (add `look.js` and `arena.js` to it), and send screenshots.

## Review Focus

- A screen rotated with Settings › Orientation (`appRot`): picking must still hit the right tile.
- Resizing during a fight (portrait to landscape): the camera refits and the overlay lines up.
- A battle started straight from the camp or a mission, with no dungeon scene built yet:
  `look.js` must not depend on `EX` or `X3`.
- The tab is hidden and comes back (a zero-size canvas): no crash, and it redraws.
- The debug menu while in battle: sliders change the arena live.

  `xDbgToggle`, `xDbgClose`, and the fps readout

**Interfaces:**

- `xLitShader` maps the AO texture with the uniforms `uXAOScale` (float, world size) and `uXAOOrg`
  (vec2), both in `XLITU`.
  - The dungeon sets them to `XN*XCS` and `(0,0)`.
- `xLitAll(root)` takes the root to traverse. `explore.js` passes `X3.group`.
- `xFpsTick(dt, renderer)` replaces the `EX` / `X3` reads. The every-120-frames `xLitAll` stays in
  `explore.js`.
- `lookUse(aoTex, aoScale, aoOrg)` sets the shared AO uniforms before a screen draws.
- `xDbgApply('bake')` calls `xBakeAO()` if `EX` exists and `arenaBakeAO()` if a battle is showing.

### Task 2: 3D board, cave and camera

**Files:**

- Create: `game/arena.js`.
- Modify: `game/battle.js` (`resizeView`, `proj`, `nearK`, `scaleAt`, `render`, `drawCave`
  removed), `game/index.html` (`#board3d`), `game/style.css`.

**Interfaces:**

- `arenaInit()` creates `AR = {renderer, scene, cam, ...}` on `#board3d`. It is called once, from
  `resizeView`.
- `arenaBuild(depth)` is called when `B.depth` or the battle changes. It builds:
  - the instanced hex prisms (radius .96, height `SLAB`)
  - the cave floor ring and walls, from the area's `stone` painters
  - the area light and the lamp
- `arenaFit(w, h)` sets the base camera. It has the dungeon's tilt (about 48°) and fov, and its
  distance is solved so every `hexCorners(t,1)` plus a y = 2.2 headroom fits within the canvas
  (portrait margin 12 px, landscape 36 px).
- `arenaProject(wx, y, wz) -> [x, y]` gives canvas pixels from `AR.cam` (the camera as drawn this
  frame).
- `proj` = `arenaProject`.
- `scaleAt(wx, wz)` = the pixel distance between `proj(wx,0,wz)` and `proj(wx,0,wz+1)`.
- `View.iy` = sin(the camera's pitch at the board centre).
- `arenaTiles(fills)`: `render()` passes `[{t, fill, glow}]`. These become the instance colours,
  with glow as emissive.
- `arenaDraw(dt)` steps the camera, then calls `lookRender`.

### Task 3: Units as 3D sprites

**Interfaces:**

- `drawSprite(ctx, u, T, S, o)` no longer paints. It calls `arenaUnit(u, spr, o)` and returns the
  same `{x, top, H, k}`, measured by projecting the sprite's feet and head.
- `arenaUnit` keeps a pooled `THREE.Sprite` per unit with the flip / flash / tint / alpha
  materials, and a ground shadow.
- `arenaEndFrame()` hides the sprites of units not seen this frame.
- `DRAW.rock` and `DRAW.wall` become lit 3D blocks through `arenaBlock(u, kind, col)`. Their
  bars stay 2D.

### Task 4: Effects and spell lights

### Task 7: Auto quality and the fps readout in battle

**Interfaces:**

- `lookQuality(dt, renderer)` keeps a rolling 60-frame average.
  - Above 20 ms, step down: pixel ratio 2 → 1.5 → 1 → .75, then SSAO off, then spell lights 4 → 2.
  - Below 13 ms for 3 s, step back up.
- `#xFps` (or the same readout in battle) shows `q<step>`.
