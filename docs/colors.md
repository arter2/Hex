# Hexmancers: the six colors

The design brief for each color: what belongs to it, where its look and feel
come from, and what it plays like. Every new card, hero, enemy, area and piece
of gear should draw on its color's list. A color is never just one theme: each
has several sub-themes, and some ideas are shared between two colors on
purpose (see *Shared ground* at the end).

The game keeps its color names and adds the brief's color word to them:

| Name in the game | Code name | Card color |
|---|---|---|
| Red · Fire | `fire` | `#ff6a3d` |
| Blue · Frost | `frost` | `#6fd6ff` |
| White · Light | `light` | `#fff0b3` |
| Green · Verdant | `verdant` | `#6fdc7a` |
| Yellow · Storm | `storm` | `#ffe24d` |
| Black · Shadow | `shadow` | `#e0588f` |

## Principles

- **Think abstract.** A color is a feeling and a world, not one element. Weather
  shows it best: **storms are Yellow, wind is White, hurricanes are Blue,
  cyclones are Red, fog is Black.**
- **Every color has its own version of a thing.** Overlap is fine as long as it
  is that color's take: wild wolves are Green, white wolves are White, dark
  wolves are Black, winter wolves are Blue.
- **Mythology from all over the world.** Cards, creatures and heroes should be
  a large and varied set drawn from many cultures, not one per color.

## Red: fire and the mountain

**Elements and places:** fire, volcanoes, lava, mountains, earth, explosions,
cyclones. Purification through flame.

**Crafts and know-how:** blacksmithing, forging, mining, guns, rockets.

**Creatures:** dragons, wyverns, gremlins, goblins, foxes, coyotes, griffins,
the phoenix, shishi (lion-dogs), duende, vampires, the minotaur, the bull
(Taurus),
yokai, kitsune, oni.

**Peoples and cultures (inspiration):** dwarf-like, Native American-like,
Japanese-like, Chinese-like, Tibetan-like, mountain peoples, exo-like *(?)*.

**Food and drink:** meat, wine.

**Gameplay sub-themes (draft):**
- **Burn:** damage over time; flame that purifies.
- **Explosions and gunpowder:** splash damage, bombs, rockets, guns.
- **The forge:** armor, heavy weapons, crafting and upgrading gear.
- **Mining and earth:** stone, tremors, digging up loot.
- **Beasts of the mountain:** dragons, wyverns and griffins as big summons;
  foxes, kitsune and coyotes as quick tricksters.
- **Fury:** spend your own HP for more power (oni, minotaur).

## Blue: water, ice and the great unknown

**Elements and places:** water, ice, snow, rivers, lakes, the ocean, floods,
hurricanes, islands, dams, ships. Cleanliness, purification through water.
The great unknown.

**Magic:** sorcery, enchantment.

**Creatures:** merpeople, fish, sea monsters, the leviathan, yeti, snowmen,
wolves, kappa.

**Peoples and cultures (inspiration):** pirates, Caribbean-like,
Eskimo/Inuit-like, Norse-like.

**Gameplay sub-themes (draft):**
- **Freeze:** stop enemies and cancel their attacks.
- **Tide and flood:** push enemies around, wash over a whole row, cleanse
  curses and poison.
- **Sorcery and enchantment:** draw more cards, copy or empower spells.
- **The deep:** huge sea-monster summons, the leviathan.
- **Pirates and the sea:** plunder (more gold and loot), cannons, crews.
- **Winter packs:** wolves and yeti, hunting together.

## White: knowledge, faith and human achievement

**Ideals:** purity, conquest, safety, the divine, sacred lands.

**Knowledge:** libraries, books, alchemy, medicine, salves (healing creams),
astronomy.

**Achievement:** architecture, fortresses, marble, stone, bricks, temples,
human achievement.

**Elements:** wind.

**People:** humans, doctors, clerics, knights, crusaders.

**Creatures and figures:** owls, Athena, Pegasus, centaurs.

**Peoples and cultures (inspiration):** British-like, Roman-like, the church,
knighthood, the crusades.

**Gameplay sub-themes (draft):**
- **Healing and medicine:** doctors and clerics, cures.
- **Knighthood and conquest:** shields, armor, charges, an army of humans.
- **Knowledge:** books, libraries and alchemy: learn recipes, see what is
  coming, upgrade cards.
- **Safety and the fortress:** walls, towers, fortresses and temples that
  stay on the board; barriers, guarding allies, taking hits for others.
- **The divine:** blessings, revival, judgment on the wicked.
- **Wind and the sky:** Pegasus, owls; speed and evasion.

## Green: nature, the forest and the wild

**Places:** forests (tropical, British, medieval), rainforest, large trees,
bogs, highlands, cabins, forest villages.

**Nature:** vines, mushrooms, fungus, mold, poison, natural medicine.

**People and figures:** elves, druids, shamans, tree people, ents, mountain
men, fairies, the Green Knight, King Arthur, Gwyn *(Gwyn ap Nudd?)*, Tapio,
Demeter, Pan.

**Creatures:** forest animals, deer, moose, bears, big cats, monkeys, apes,
lizards, snakes, ants, thallids, sasquatch, tanuki.

**Peoples and cultures (inspiration):** Elven, Germanic, Celtic-like,
Mayan-like.

**Hunting:** hunting, bows, arrows.

**Gameplay sub-themes (draft):**
- **Growth:** summons that multiply (Brood, thallids, ants), trees that grow.
- **Poison and fungus:** poison, spores, mold.
- **The hunt:** bows and arrows, big cats and bears, pouncing.
- **Natural medicine:** healing over time, regrowth.
- **The old forest:** ents and tree folk; walls of roots and vines.
- **Fey trickery:** fairies, tanuki, Pan: slow, confuse.

## Yellow: lightning, sand and greed

**Elements and places:** electricity, storms, the desert, dryness, sand, the
Sahara.

**Science and machines:** electrification, chemistry, chemicals, 16th-century
science, flying machines, traps, bronze, golems.

**Greed:** coin, greed.

**Effects:** paralysis.

**Creatures and figures:** birds, lizards, snakes, scarabs, camels, the
manticore, the chimera, Foo dogs, djinn, ifrit, Horus, the chupacabra, and
mythical creatures from many cultures: the dybbuk *(best guess for "dybics")*,
Bahamut *(best guess for "baebue")*, the si'lat *("sila")*, Falak *("falek")*.

**Peoples and cultures (inspiration):** Middle Eastern, Persian-like,
Bedouin-like, Arabian-like, Egyptian-like.

**Gameplay sub-themes (draft):**
- **Lightning:** paralyze and stun, chain between enemies.
- **Machines and science:** golems, flying machines, traps, turrets.
- **Greed:** coin: more gold, cards that cost gold or grow with it.
- **Chemistry:** potions, chemicals, transmutation.
- **The desert:** sandstorms, dryness, scarabs and snakes.
- **Wishes:** djinn and ifrit; powerful but with a catch.

## Black: death, horror and dark magic

**Death and disease:** plague, death, zombies, skeletons, ghosts, ghouls.

**Elements:** fog.

**Dark magic:** dark magic, witches, the cauldron, voodoo, tarot, curses.

**Evil:** evil, the devil, demons, satanic.

**Places:** bogs, caves, dungeons.

**Creatures:** vampires, werewolves, bugs, beetles, spiders.

**Rot and poison:** mold, poison, chemistry.

**Horror:** horror, torture.

**Gameplay sub-themes (draft):**
- **Undead:** summons that rise again, skeleton and zombie hordes.
- **Plague and rot:** disease that spreads from enemy to enemy, poison, mold.
- **Curses and hexes:** witches, voodoo, the cauldron; enemies take more
  damage.
- **Pacts with demons:** power for a price (your HP, your cards).
- **Blood:** vampires, lifesteal.
- **Fate:** tarot cards: random strong effects.
- **Horror:** fear and confusion; ghosts that pass through walls.

## Shared ground

Some ideas appear in two colors. That is good, as long as each color treats
them its own way:

| Idea | Colors | How they differ |
|---|---|---|
| Vampires | Red, Black | Red: fiery, passionate, Spanish/duende folklore. Black: undead blood drinkers. |
| Poison | Green, Black | Green: natural (snakes, plants). Black: plague, chemistry, curses. |
| Mold and fungus | Green, Black | Green: growth, thallids. Black: rot and decay. |
| Bogs | Green, Black | Green: living wetland. Black: haunted swamp. |
| Chemistry | Yellow, Black | Yellow: science and invention. Black: poisons and the cauldron. |
| Lizards and snakes | Green, Yellow | Green: jungle and forest. Yellow: desert. |
| Purification | Red, Blue | Red: through flame. Blue: through water. |
| Weather | all | Storms Yellow, wind White, hurricanes Blue, cyclones Red, fog Black. |
| Wolves | all | Wild wolves Green, white wolves White, dark wolves Black, winter wolves Blue. |
| Mountains | Red, Green | Red: volcanoes, mines. Green: highlands, mountain men. |
| Stone and earth | Red, White | Red: raw earth, mining. White: marble and architecture. |

## The two neutral colors

Gray and Brown go in any deck. They are not one of the six; they sit beside them.

### Gray: the normal type

Gray is the "Normal" type, as in Pokémon: creatures that make sense in more
than one color, so any deck can use them and they still feel interesting.

- Creatures that fit several colors at once: suits of armor, golems, guardians,
  constructs, familiars, mounts.
- Mythical heroes and champions from every culture: the warriors, wanderers and
  legends who do not belong to one element.
- Plain but characterful: the strength of Gray is being reliable and flexible,
  not flashy.

### Brown: technology

Brown is technology, from all the different races in the game. It plays like
enchantments, artifacts and instants in Magic: The Gathering.

- Buff potions.
- Temporary enchantments on your creatures, or on cards in your hand.
- Artifacts and devices: turrets, engines, tools, contraptions.
- Instants: quick one-off effects.
- Every race brings its own tech: dwarven forges, gnomish clockwork, elven
  crystal lenses, desert alchemy, sea-folk diving gear, and so on.
- Deck tools (Brown only):
  - Search: pull a random card of a kind from your deck into your hand
    (a random spell, a random creature, a random card of one rune...).
  - Reshuffle: shuffle your whole hand back into the deck and draw a new one.
  - Keep and reshuffle: choose cards to keep, shuffle the rest back and draw
    the same number again.

### Rules for the neutral colors

Gray:
- No weakness: Gray deals and takes even damage against every color.
- Veteran: a Gray creature gets +10% power for each turn it survives.
- Guardian: armor and guard creatures step in and take hits aimed at you.
- Legend: Gray mythical heroes make your other Gray creatures stronger while they stand.

Brown:
- True instants: tap one in your hand during the fight and it fires at once
  (no Spell screen, no queue slot).
- Equipment: gear attaches to one of your creatures; if it dies the gear drops
  and your next summon picks it up.
- Tinker: an upgraded card in your hand keeps the upgrade for the whole battle.
- Unstable: goblin and gremlin tech is stronger but has a 1 in 5 chance to misfire.
- Recharge: some tech refills the uses of your Charge cards.
- Race tech: each race's tech gets stronger for every other card of that race in
  your hand (+10% each).

### Aces and the Sidearm

An **Ace** is a finisher that is not used right away. Cast from the queue, it
loads into your **Sidearm**, which gets its own button (♠ Ace) beside Fire and
Cast. You fire it when you need it.

- Only **Ace Enchantments** build it up, not every card. They come from every
  color, and each one adds power (up to ×3), may change how the Ace hits (a row,
  every enemy, a blast around the target, or two shots), and adds its color's
  effect (burn, freeze, stun, poison, drain, curse, mend...).
- An enchantment cast with no Ace loaded waits for the next one.
- One Ace at a time: loading another replaces it. It is gone when the fight ends.
- Each of the six colors has 3 Aces and 3 Ace Enchantments; Brown has 12 Aces
  and 15 Enchantments (guns, ammo, scopes and barrels).
