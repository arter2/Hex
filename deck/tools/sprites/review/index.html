Hexmancers Sprite Review

Every character, humanoid, monster, boss and hero, redrawn at 128 × 128 from the concept sheets and scored on eight categories (max 80). Rounds 1 and 2 used three models; round 3 uses five stricter Opus judges, each a specialist (pixel technique, character design, animation, arcade art direction, in-game readability). A sprite passes the panel at an average of 72. Your call is final: approve it for the game, or send it back for another round with a note.
