Custom

Queue any 3 cards; they cast left to right. Combos: copies (Double); one rune, color and type (Flush); one rune and ranks in a row (Straight); or a secret recipe. The rune is the letter on the card (✱ fits any); cards matching your queue's rune are outlined. 4 cards of one rune in hand open a 4th slot. Each empty slot draws 1 extra card next time.
