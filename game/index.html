Spell

Queue any 3 cards; they cast left to right. Combo rulesCombos: copies (Double); 3 different cards of one rune and color (Flush: they merge into one attack with all their power +25% and every effect they carry); one rune and ranks in a row (Straight); runes in order left to right like A, B, C (Rune Run: Haste and Courage, +30% cards and wand, for 8s); three of one rune (Rune Set: wand ×2.5 for 5s); or a secret recipe. ✧ Instants (Brown) never go in the queue: tap one to use it at once. ✧ Deck tools (Search, Keep, Reshuffle) go in a slot: double-tap one there to use it, and that slot is shut for this hand (a Reshuffle shuts it for 2). ♠ Aces load into your Sidearm; fire them with the Ace button. The rune is the letter on the card (✱ fits any); cards matching your queue's rune are outlined. ᚱ Rune Surge: 3 queued cards of one rune open a 4th slot, and only a card of that rune (or ✱) fits it. Your hand holds 6 cards at most. When your deck runs out, your used cards are shuffled back in. Hold a card to read it in full.

Lighting

Texture-pixel lightingOn: light is worked out once per texture pixel, so it falls across walls and floors in crisp art-pixel steps. Off: smooth light.
Light bands0 keeps light smooth. Otherwise light is rounded to this many levels per full brightness, for a banded pixel-art look.

Normal maps

Ambient occlusion

Baked AOFloors darken toward the walls and into corners; walls and pillars darken toward their feet. Worked out once per floor, so it costs almost nothing.
Baked AO strengthHow dark the shading gets. 1 is about the old look.
Baked AO reachHow far the shading spreads from a wall, in tiles.
Screen-space AOShades every crease in the frame (props, steps, doors, monsters too). Draws the frame twice more, so it is heavy on phones.
Screen-space AO strengthHow dark the creases get.
Screen-space AO reachHow far each crease's shading spreads, in tiles.

Dungeon camera

AngleHow steeply the dungeon camera looks down, in degrees. 48 is normal. Higher sees further up the room and walls in front hide less; lower shows more of the wall faces.
DistanceHow far the camera sits from you, in tiles (a tall phone screen pulls it back a little more). 9.4 is normal.
Field of view (lens)The lens, in degrees. 46 is normal. Wider shows more around you with more depth; narrower is flatter.
Aim aheadPoints the camera further up the screen (above 0) so you stand lower and see more ahead, in tiles.
Look-aheadHow far the camera leads you as you walk. 0 keeps you dead centre; 1 is normal.
